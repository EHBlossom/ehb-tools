📋 Quick Reference — 4 Tenses at a Glance

🔵 Present

単数There is
複数There are
否定（単）There isn't
否定（複）There aren't
疑問（単）Is there?
疑問（複）Are there?
「〜があります・います」

🟡 Past

単数There was
複数There were
否定（単）There wasn't
否定（複）There weren't
疑問（単）Was there?
疑問（複）Were there?
「〜がありました・いました」

🔵 will be

単数There will be
複数There will be
否定There won't be
疑問Will there be?
「〜があるでしょう」予測・即決
